# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PRN232 instructors, teaching assistants, and course operators who need to manage practical exam sessions, lab assignments, submissions, test cases, grading progress, and exports from a single internal tool. Users are usually working under course deadlines and need the interface to make grading state, missing work, and next actions obvious without adding ceremony.

Authentication exists in the codebase but is not a current product emphasis. Future design work should treat access control as secondary unless the task specifically targets login, roles, or account flows.

## Product Purpose

PRN232 Auto Grader centralizes automated grading for PE exams and lab programming assignments. It helps staff create grading sessions, configure assignments and test cases, monitor submitted work, review results, and export scores reliably. Success means instructors can move from setup to grading to export with fewer manual checks, clearer progress visibility, and fewer opportunities for grading mistakes.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, clear, efficient. The existing Zapier-inspired brand direction should be preserved: approachable, warm, and practical, with orange used as the primary action and status accent. The product should feel more like a trustworthy operations tool than a decorative marketing surface.

## Anti-references

Avoid cold developer-console aesthetics, heavy dark dashboards, playful classroom theming, decorative gradients, and over-animated interactions. Avoid redesigns that replace the current warm white, orange, and neutral system without a specific reason. Avoid making auth the center of the experience unless explicitly requested.

## Design Principles

Make grading state visible at a glance.

Keep workflows direct: setup, upload, review, export.

Preserve the current brand system while tightening consistency.

Use density where it helps scanning, but keep primary actions easy to find.

Design for operational confidence: clear labels, recoverable actions, and explicit error states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, readable table and form layouts, reduced-motion alternatives, and status communication that does not rely on color alone.
